Every page and how to reach it

A section button opens the page you last used in that section; press it again for the next page. GLO is the exception: from another page it always opens the mixer (at the mixer page you used last), even if you were last on the master bus. SELECT also turns between a section's pages and stops at the first and last. Changing tracks resets EDIT to its first page.

Drum pads: every knob

On DRUMS, tap a pad's black key, then press EDIT. The pad's knobs fill pages of four in the order below; the last EDIT page is the KIT page. Every knob is 0–127 except the switches. Rev and Dly are the pad's own sends into the reverb and delay; the mixer's DRUMS REVERB and DELAY sends then set how much of them reaches the effects.

  • Dist picks the distortion that Drive feeds: Diode, Clip, SAT, BFZ, PDIST, Fold, Crush.
  • P.Dpth and Pitch on the 909 BD set the depth and amount of the kick's pitch drop. Tail on the 909 CP is the clap's reverb-like tail. Snappy is the snare wires' level.
  • The 909 and 808 keep their own knob settings. Switching kits and back restores the previous settings.
  • The 909 and 808 are level-matched for equivalent pads. The 808's accent is stronger, about 6 dB against the 909's 3, as on a real 808; its KIT page's Accent sets how much.
  • Pads triggered by the sequencer play at a normal level; accented steps play at full level, shaped by the KIT page's Accent.

User presets are stored apart from the project, so loading or saving the project doesn't change them.
