HOME is a 16-column clip grid for the four track rows. Make a couple of patterns first, then practice changing only the bass while the drums carry on. You don't have to stop playback to launch a clip.

Launch a scene or an individual clip

Press HOME from a track page. A filled clip is playing; a blinking clip is waiting to start. White keys address pattern columns; black keys 1–4 address the DRUMS, T2, T3, T4 rows.

Set quantized launching in SONG → K3 LAUNCH: BAR (default), BEAT, or NOW. BEAT gives a quicker change and NOW is immediate. When clips are mixed from different patterns, STEPS and EDIT go to that track's current clip. The top pattern number only shows the last scene picked. Check the row on HOME before an edit.

Play an effect without writing it into the loop

On HOME, hold a black perform key. It acts while held, and the normal mix returns on release. Turn K1 while holding an effect to adjust its setting where shown.

Stutter and Tape Stop borrow the delay's memory, so expect the delay tail to disappear while they're held. Those effects are unavailable while sampling. In the browser a latched key stays on until you release the latch; a normal press is better for a quick throw.

Build a set with SONG or follow actions

For a fixed arrangement: press HOME again from the scene grid to get SONG. Use up to 32 entries, each 1–16 bars. SELECT moves the cursor; white pattern keys set the cursor's scene and advance, adding an entry at the end. K1 changes the entry's pattern, K2 its bars, K3 launch timing, K4 turns SONG on or off. OCT+ copies an entry, OCT− deletes it. PLAY starts at the cursor on SONG, otherwise from the beginning. The arrangement loops.

For patterns that advance themselves: on HOME hold your scene's white key and use K1 for NEXT, PREV, FIRST or RANDOM and K2 for how many bars (1–16). Try pattern 1 NEXT after 4 bars and pattern 2 FIRST after 4. Manually launching a scene restarts its clock. SONG takes precedence when enabled.

To make two scenes with different kits or synths, select SETUP → SOUNDS → OWN. This stores per-pattern sounds, not a different mixer or master for each scene. See Setup.
