Start by balancing the tracks. Add sends next. Adjust the master only after the mix works. MASTER is a listening-volume knob after the mastering chain; it doesn't improve headroom inside the chain.

Read and adjust the master bus

Hold SEL and press GLO to jump to CHAIN. GLO advances its pages; SELECT can also move back into the four mixer pages. Signal order: EQ → COMP → DJ filter → TAPE → STEREO → GAIN → LIMITER → MASTER.

Master controls in order
PageControls
CHAINPreset (CLEAN, GLUE, PUNCH, WARM, WIDE, LOUD, LO-FI), GAIN into the limiter (0 to +12 dB), CEILING (−0.3 dB initially), and BYPASS. A star marks a changed preset. BYPASS still keeps a limiter at 0 dB GAIN.
EQ / EQ 2Low, mid, high (±12 dB), LOW CUT; then the band frequencies and MID Q. Low Q means a wider band.
COMP / COMP 2Threshold, ratio, attack, release; then makeup, parallel MIX, kick-triggered PUMP, and a reduction readout. Ratio 1:1 turns compression off.
TAPEDRIVE rounds peaks, WARMTH adds even harmonics, TONE rolls off highs, MIX blends.
STEREOWIDTH, BASS MONO, BALANCE, correlation readout. 100% width leaves it as-is. Lower frequencies below BASS MONO center; a correlation below zero warns of cancellation in mono.
LIMITGAIN, CEILING, RELEASE, limiter ON/OFF. Turning it off does not stop a hot mix from clipping.

How to compare: loop a representative bar. Check input/output peaks and compressor/limiter reduction on CHAIN. The LUFS display shows short-term (3 seconds) and momentary (0.4 seconds) loudness. Try CLEAN against BYPASS first so loudness doesn't decide for you. If the limiter is constantly taking a large bite, remove GAIN or rebalance the tracks. The example-groove readings in the source manual aren't targets for every song.

Track inserts are separate from the shared master. For instrument effects, use SEL + FX on T2–T4. This preview does not add per-track EQ/compression or multiband mastering. To hear the bus more loudly while preserving its behavior, raise MASTER; to drive the chain harder or change loudness, use GAIN and listen to the reduction.
