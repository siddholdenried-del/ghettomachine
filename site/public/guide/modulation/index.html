Modulation in depth

Modulation moves a sound while it plays, without you touching a knob. GhettoMachine has five sources of movement: LFO 1 (one per track, can follow the tempo), LFO 2 (one per note), the MOD ENV (a third envelope per note), the four MACRO knobs, and step locks written by the sequencer. On T2–T4 the MATRIX connects LFO 1, LFO 2, the three envelopes, velocity, the note, a random value and the macros to 17 destinations, six routes per sound. This chapter covers where each one lives, what it can reach, how far it moves things, and recipes to start from.

Where the modulation pages are

Each section button opens the page you last used in that section. Press it again for the next page. The quickest way to a page is to press its button, then turn SELECT to move between that section's pages. SELECT stops at the first and last page, so turning it fully right always lands on the last page.

LFO 1: one per track

LFO 1 moves the whole track at once: every note of a chord sweeps together. It is the only modulation source that can lock to the tempo. Its settings belong to the project, not to the sound, so changing PRESETS keeps the LFO as it is.

LFO 1 on the drums

  1. On DRUMS, tap the black key of the pad you want to move (CH, for example).
  2. Press LFO. Turn K4 DEST right from OFF through that pad's knobs: for the 909 CH, Decay, Level, Tune, Drive and Pan. Switches (Dist, Sound) and sends (Rev, Dly) aren't on the list.
  3. Set K3 DEPTH. At ±64 the knob moves up to 64 of its 0–127 range.

The drum LFO is read once at each hit of that pad, starting from the knob's value or that step's lock. A hit keeps that value while it rings, so an S&H or a fast LFO gives each hit a different setting. The drums have one LFO, for one pad at a time. Turning DEST while a different pad is selected moves the LFO to that pad. Hits played into the FM-1 over MIDI skip the LFO and step locks.

LFO 2: one per note (T2–T4)

Every note gets its own LFO 2. In a chord each voice can wobble on its own, and a note that starts later starts its own cycle. LFO 2 does nothing by itself: it only reaches the sound through a MATRIX slot whose SOURCE is LFO 2.

LFO 2 is part of the sound: it's saved in user presets and changes with PRESETS. Its four knobs can be locked per step and recorded as motion.

The MOD ENV and the other envelopes

ENV has three pages: AMP ENV, FILTER ENV and MOD ENV. Each has ATTACK, DECAY and RELEASE from 1 ms to 5.0 s, and SUSTAIN 0–127. Every note runs its own copy of all three.

  • AMP ENV shapes the volume. It is also a matrix source.
  • FILTER ENV moves the cutoff by EDIT → FILTER → ENV AMT (−64 to +63). It is also a matrix source, so one envelope can move the filter and, for example, the FM amount.
  • MOD ENV has no job until a matrix slot uses it. Unless a preset sets it, it starts at ATTACK 1 ms, DECAY 528 ms, SUSTAIN 0 and RELEASE 157 ms, so it rises at once and falls away within about half a second. Point it at FM, SHAPER, PITCH, OSC 2 or anything else in the list below.

Envelope times are 1 ms at 0, 81 ms at 32, 641 ms at 64, 2.2 s at 96 and 5.0 s at 127.

The MATRIX: six routes per sound

Press LFO and turn SELECT fully right. The page lists all six slots, and the one on the knobs is highlighted.

  1. K1 SLOT picks MOD 1 to MOD 6. The screen reader reads the slot as, for example, “LFO 2 > PITCH +4”, or OFF.
  2. K2 SOURCE picks what moves.
  3. K3 DEST picks what is moved.
  4. K4 AMOUNT sets how far, from −64 to +63. A slot does nothing while its SOURCE is OFF or its AMOUNT is 0.

Macros: one knob, many moves

Press EDIT and turn SELECT fully right to MACRO; on a SAMPLER track it is also the last EDIT page. The four knobs, 0–127 and 0 at first, do nothing until a matrix slot uses MACRO 1–4 as its SOURCE. The MACRO page shows each macro's position and what the matrix connects it to.

Several factory sounds already have MACRO 1 connected, so turn K1 on their MACRO page:

Modulation per step: locks

A lock gives one step its own value for one knob. The sound jumps to that value when the step plays and goes back to the knob's own value at the track's next step that has notes and no lock for that knob.

  1. On T2–T4, open STEPS (SEQ) and hold a lit step's white key.
  2. Keep holding it and press EDIT, ENV or LFO. While a step is held, SELECT turns the pages on those three sections, so you can reach any page.
  3. Turn a knob. Its card shows an L and turns pale violet once you stop turning: that value is locked to the held step. Let go of the step.
  • On T2–T4 you can lock the synth knobs on EDIT and ENV, the macros, LFO 2's knobs and the matrix AMOUNTs. These answer NOT LOCKABLE: TYPE, LEVEL, VOICES (UNISON), LEGATO, the matrix's SOURCE and DEST, and the sampler's START, END, LOOP, LOOP START, ROOT, TUNE and RECORD AT. LFO 1, the arpeggiator, the mixer and the effects can't be locked either.
  • On DRUMS, open EDIT for the pad (the white keys are its steps there too), hold a lit step and turn one of the pad's knobs. You can also hold the step on STEPS and then tap EDIT. On STEPS itself the knobs are LENGTH, RATE, SWING and TEMPO, not locks. The KIT page and the Sound switches can't be locked.
  • Each part holds 16 locks. When they run out the screen says NO LOCK LEFT. On DRUMS a step's CHANCE also uses one of the 16. On T2–T4 chance is stored in the step itself.
  • Hold a step and press OCT− to clear its locks (and put its chance back to 100%).

Locking a macro or a matrix AMOUNT gives you one-step timbre changes: route MACRO 2 to FM and RING, then lock MACRO 2 high on the off-beats only.

Record knob moves as motion

Tap REC so it lights, press PLAY, open a sound page and turn a knob. Each step the playhead reaches while you turn gets a lock with the knob's value then. Undo with EDIT + OCT−. Turns close together undo as one.

  • Recorded on T2–T4: the synth knobs on EDIT and ENV, the macros, LFO 2's knobs and the matrix AMOUNTs. TYPE, LEVEL and the sampler's START, END, LOOP, LOOP START, ROOT, TUNE and RECORD AT aren't recorded. On DRUMS: the selected pad's EDIT knobs, but not the KIT page or the 808's Sound switches.
  • Careful: recording also captures turns of VOICES (UNISON), LEGATO and the matrix's SOURCE and DEST, which you can't lock by hand. Leave those alone while REC is lit and the transport runs, or clear the locks afterwards with a held step + OCT−.
  • Not recorded: LFO 1, ARP, PRESETS, the mixer, the effects and the master bus.
  • A sweep across 16 steps uses all 16 locks of the part. Record short gestures, or clear the old locks first.
  • Recording only happens while REC is lit and the transport runs. Stopped, the knob changes the sound itself.

Recipes

Each recipe starts from a factory sound on T2 unless it says otherwise. Numbers without a unit are knob positions (0–127, or the signed AMOUNT); values with a unit are what the screen shows.

  1. Tempo wobble bass. SQR BASS. EDIT → FILTER: CUTOFF about 40, RESO about 70. LFO 1: SHAPE SINE, DEPTH +40, DEST CUTOFF. LFO 1 MORE: SYNC TEMPO, RETRIG BAR. Back on LFO 1, set RATE to 1/8. Try 1/8T or 1/4. for a broken rhythm.
  2. Any destination, in time. On LFO 1 MORE, SYNC TEMPO. LFO 1: RATE 1/4, DEPTH 0. MATRIX: MOD 1 LFO 1 → PAN +40, MOD 2 LFO 1 → FM +20. Because the matrix takes LFO 1 before its DEPTH, the LFO reaches PAN and FM, which aren't on LFO 1's own DEST list.
  3. Delayed vibrato for pads. SOFT PAD. LFO 2: SHAPE SINE, RATE about 5 Hz, FADE IN about 600 ms, RESTART EACH NOTE. MATRIX: LFO 2 → PITCH +8, about three-quarters of a semitone.
  4. Plucked FM. SINE KEYS. EDIT → OSC 2: FM 10. MOD ENV: ATTACK 0, DECAY about 32 (81 ms), SUSTAIN 0. MATRIX: MOD ENV → FM +40, VELOCITY → FM +20. Harder notes get brighter. Compare FM BELL.
  5. Velocity to brightness. MATRIX: VELOCITY → CUTOFF +30, and lower CUTOFF to match. Soft notes are darker. Set a step's velocity with K2 while holding it on STEPS.
  6. Keyboard-dependent tone. MATRIX: NOTE → DRIVE +20 so high notes get grittier, and NOTE → PAN +30 so low notes sit left and high notes right.
  7. No two notes the same. MATRIX: RANDOM → PAN +25, RANDOM → CUTOFF +10, RANDOM → PITCH +3 for a slight detune per note.
  8. Vibrato that speeds up. MOD ENV: ATTACK about 96 (2.2 s), SUSTAIN 127. MATRIX: MOD ENV → LFO 2 RATE +40, LFO 2 → PITCH +6.
  9. One performance knob. SAW LEAD. EDIT → SHAPER: type FOLD, AMOUNT about 10. MATRIX: MACRO 1 → CUTOFF +45, MACRO 1 → SHAPER +30, MACRO 1 → RESO +20. Now EDIT → MACRO K1 opens the filter and the folder together. Record it with REC armed, or lock it per step.
  10. Random hi-hat decays (DRUMS). Tap the CH pad. LFO 1: SHAPE S&H, DEPTH +30, DEST CH Decay. LFO 1 MORE: SYNC TEMPO, RETRIG BAR. LFO 1: RATE 1/16. Every closed hat gets its own length.
  11. Moving sample start (SAMPLER). On a SAMPLER track with a sample: LFO 1: SHAPE SAW UP, DEPTH +50, DEST START. LFO 1 MORE: SYNC TEMPO, RETRIG BAR. LFO 1: RATE 1 BAR. Each note in the bar starts further into the sample.

Turn a source's AMOUNT down to 0 when you're done with it rather than leaving it at a value you can't hear: a route you forgot about can make a later sound behave strangely. Every sound parameter with its range
