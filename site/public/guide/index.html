Quick start

Make a beat and a bass line in GhettoMachine, then use this guide to look up the controls. This edition covers the current FM-1 browser build: genre drum kits, one drum track, three synth tracks, and up to 128 steps per part. Browser project saves don't survive a reload. See what gets saved before you start a project you want to keep.

Make your first complete pattern

This makes one bar of drums and a bass part. A white key is a step on STEPS and a note on KEYS. A black key is a drum pad on DRUMS, a page selector on instrument STEPS, or a note on KEYS. The screen tells you which job the keys are doing.

  1. Start and listen. Open the browser groovebox, click Switch on, then press PLAY / STOP to hear the demo. Press it again to stop. On FM-1 hardware, use MASTER for a comfortable listening level; the browser also has a separate Mute button.
  2. Pick a spare pattern. Save any work you want to keep. On the HOME scene grid (press HOME from a track page), choose an unused pattern with a white key. A scene selects that pattern on all four tracks. Stay stopped so the change takes effect now. Return to a track with SEQ. Don't assume the demo pattern is blank. To clear one, see Erase steps, parts and whole patterns.
  3. Lay in the kick. Turn ALGORITHM to DRUMS. Turn PRESETS to ELECTRO, press SEQ, and set K1 LENGTH to 16, K2 RATE to 1/16, and K4 TEMPO to around 120. Tap the black key for Kick (black 1), then light white steps 1, 5, 9, 13. If a target step is already lit, leave it on rather than toggling it off.
  4. Add the backbeat and hats. Pick Snare (black 2) and light 5 and 13. Pick Closed Hat (black 7) and light 3, 7, 11, 15. The step lights follow whichever pad you picked; changing which pad you see does not erase the others. Press PLAY for a bar, then stop.
  5. Put down bass notes. Select T2 with ALGORITHM and choose a bass preset such as SQR BASS if you see it. Press SEQ until you see KEYS, then play a low note you like, using OCT− if needed. Press SEQ until the screen says STEPS. Turn SELECT to PART GROOVE and set the T2 length and rate to 16 and 1/16 as well. Light steps 1, 4, 7, 11; newly lit steps take the last note you played. Turn SELECT back to STEP NOTES. Hold step 11 and turn K1 NOTE seven clicks up for a fifth above the other notes. Let go only after turning the knob.
  6. Listen, balance, save. Press PLAY. If the bass covers the drums, open the mixer with GLO, find LEVEL and turn K2 down a little. On hardware, stop and tap SAVE to keep the project. In the browser, this only saves within the current session. To make a second version, return to HOME, hold SAVE and press another unused white pattern key. That copy overwrites the destination, so check the slot first.

If leaving a step early feels awkward in the browser, latch the white key with Shift-click, turn the knob, then unlatch by clicking the key again. Browser controls and keyboard shortcuts

What each control does

Panel quick reference
ControlMain jobContext that changes its job
ALGORITHMPick DRUMS, T2, T3 or T4.Or hold SEL and tap black key 1–4. Switching tracks takes you to DRUMS STEPS or instrument KEYS.
PRESETSChoose a whole kit on DRUMS, or an instrument sound on T2–T4.Hold SEL to jump synth banks. In Drum EDIT, PRESETS changes only the selected pad. On T2–T4, hold SAVE to choose a user sound slot, then release SAVE to write it.
SELECTTurn the page within a section, or move the cursor. On Drum Steps, use OCT− / OCT+ for banks.On Drum Steps it switches between PART GROOVE and DRUM HIT. On synth STEPS it selects the editing tools when no step is held.
K1–K4Turn the settings shown in the four knob cards, left to right.Held steps, the REC page and performance modifiers can change the meaning. Read the cards after holding a modifier.
PLAY / STOPStart or stop transport.With REC armed, a configured count-in starts first. PLAY during count-in cancels it.
RECTap to arm/disarm note and motion recording.Hold to open recording settings. Hold REC and press GLO to erase the selected track's part and record it again. Hold REC and press SEL to start sampling on a SAMPLER track.
SEQOpen STEPS.On T2–T4, cycle STEPS → ACID → KEYS. On DRUMS, cycle DRUM STEPS → BEAT. Hold SEQ while tapping steps to toggle accents.
EDIT / ENV / LFO / ARPEdit the sound, envelopes, LFO or arp. Press again to advance its pages.DRUMS ENV is pad MUTE. ARP is for T2–T4.
FX / GLOOpen the send-effect settings or the mixer.Hold SEL and press FX on T2–T4 to open the track's insert. Hold SEL then press GLO to jump straight to master bus CHAIN. With REC held, GLO erases the track's part instead.
HOMEScene grid, SONG, SETUP, then back to the track.On HOME, the white keys select scenes rather than steps; black keys 1–4 address rows.
OCT− / OCT+Change octave on instrument KEYS.Bank steps on STEPS, mutate/regenerate on ACID, and perform on HOME. See the shortcut table.
SAVESave the project. Save light means there are changes.Hold + pattern: copy; hold + PRESETS: save a user sound; hold + OCT−: restore the selected sound as saved.

Read the LCD while you work

  • Top band: track, page, active kit/pad/sound, pattern, tempo, running triangle and record dot. A blinking >02 indicates a queued pattern, not a stopped transport. When a song is running you may see S03 for the song slot.
  • Page area: step grid, waveform, envelope, EQ curve, or the values on the page. On HOME, it also shows each track's clips and the voice/CPU readouts.
  • Four knob cards: K1–K4 left to right, in one ruled grid. The card you are turning is violet. A pale violet card with L is locked on that step. A card on the bare background with a green gauge is a meter or readout, not adjustable.
  • Colors: violet marks what is selected (the knob being turned, the page you're on, the picked pad, matrix slot or song entry); green is for live meters. Each section keeps its own color for its name and what it draws: DRUMS in its section color, T2 green, T3 purple, T4 yellow, HOME, SONG and mixer bone, FX cyan, master bus rose. Read the page name too; color isn't necessary to follow the guide.
  • Two output meters: green, left and right before MASTER. A red cap warns that the output has reached the top. Turn down levels feeding the chain, not only the listening volume.
