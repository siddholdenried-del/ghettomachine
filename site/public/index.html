GhettoMachine

Four-track groovebox firmware. 909, 808, 606 and 707 kits, three synth or sampler tracks, and a 128-step sequencer.

GhettoMachine is BongWare custom firmware engineered by BongTech, part of BongCult. Make a beat in the browser or look up the controls for the FM-1.
