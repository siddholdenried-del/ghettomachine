# The drums' beat steps (0.5): on the drum pages the white keys of steps 1, 5, 9 and 13 of the bank shown glow when
# the step is empty (a lit step stays lit), and the screen's grid gives those steps a column of their own. T2..T4's
# step pages are as they were.  tools/build.py ../../app --host, then
#   app/build/host/ghettomachine_host app/tests/beats.txt OUTDIR   (OUTDIR must exist: beats.png is the drums' grid)
# wlit / wdim: the white keys lit / glowing, a bit a key (white 1: 1, white 5: 16, white 9: 256, white 13: 4096)
expect oldpick1 1   # (0.5: ALGORITHM keeps the page across tracks; this suite was written when it went to the track's own)
expect demode_0 1                 # (the stock 808 / 909: the DrumEngine has its own suites)
wait 600
expect trk 1
expect page 0
# ---- BD (the default beat: 1, 5, 9, 13): every beat lit, none left to glow
expect pad 0
expect wlit 4369
expect wdim 0
# ---- SD (5, 13): those lit, 1 and 9 glow
key 3 down
wait 30
key 3 up
wait 30
expect pad 1
expect wlit 4112
expect wdim 257
# ---- CH (3, 7, 11, 15): off the beat, so every beat glows
key 17 down
wait 30
key 17 up
wait 30
expect pad 7
expect wlit 17476
expect wdim 4369
shot beats.png
# ---- SEQ held: the accents (1, 9) lit, the other beats glow
press SEQ
wait 30
expect wlit 257
expect wdim 4112
release SEQ
wait 30
# ---- a step lit on a beat is lit, not glowing; off again, it glows
key 7 down
wait 30
key 7 up
wait 30
expect hit7_5 1
expect wlit 17492
expect wdim 4353
key 7 down
wait 30
key 7 up
wait 30
expect hit7_5 0
expect wdim 4369
# ---- LENGTH 6: only steps 1 and 5 are beats there
spin K1 -10
wait 30
expect len 6
expect wlit 4
expect wdim 17
# ---- LENGTH 32, the second bank (17-32, empty): its 17, 21, 25, 29 glow
spin K1 26
wait 30
expect len 32
tap OCT+
wait 30
expect wlit 0
expect wdim 4369
tap OCT-
wait 30
spin K1 -16
wait 30
expect len 16
# ---- the beats glow on the drums' other pages too (EDIT, MUTE): the keys are steps there
tap EDIT
wait 30
expect page 1
expect wdim 4369
tap ENV
wait 30
expect page 2
expect wdim 4369
press SEQ   # (0.5: home, the steps)
wait 38
release SEQ
wait 38
wait 30
expect page 0

turn ALGO 1
