# A kit change stops the kit that leaves (0.5): its tails used to ring on under the new kit's hits, two kits to render
# at once (on the FM-1 the drums took 44-48 % of a block at a scene's change, and blocks ran late). Now the old kit's
# sounds are cut as the new one comes, by PRESETS or by a scene with its own kit (SOUNDS PER PATTERN).
#   tools/build.py ../../app --host, then  app/build/host/ghettomachine_host app/tests/kitswitch.txt OUTDIR
# stem1: the drums' stem's peak since stemreset, dB x 10 (-1200: silence)
wait 600
master 4096
expect kit 0
# ---- the 909's crash (MIDI note 49 on channel 10) rings for seconds
midi 99 31 7F
wait 200
expect stemreset 1
wait 200
expect stem1 >-700
# PRESETS to the 808: the 909's crash is gone at once
turn PRESET 1
wait 60
expect kit 1
expect stemreset 1
wait 200
expect stem1 -1200
# the same kit again (a user's edit, a pattern on the same kit) cuts nothing: the 808's cymbal rings on
midi 99 31 7F
wait 200
expect stemreset 1
wait 200
expect stem1 >-700
wait 3000
