# The 808's level (0.5): each 808 sound is made up, after its drive, to the loudness of the 909 pad that plays its part
# (dsp/drum808.c k_make), so the default beat sounds as loud on either kit. Before, the 808 played it 12 dB quieter
# (short-term -31.7 LUFS against the 909's -19.5).  tools/build.py ../../app --host, then
#   app/build/host/ghettomachine_host app/tests/kit808.txt OUTDIR
# lufs: the master bus's short-term loudness (3 s), LUFS x 10, measured while a master bus page shows its meters
expect demode_0 1                 # (the stock 808 / 909: the DrumEngine has its own suites)
wait 600
master 4096
press SEL
wait 30
tap GLO
wait 30
release SEL
wait 30
expect page 12
# ---- the 909 plays the default beat (BD, SD, CH, accents on 1 and 9)
expect kit 0
expect stemreset 1
tap PLAY
wait 4500
expect lufs >-210
expect lufs <-180
tap PLAY
wait 3500
# ---- the 808 plays it at the same loudness, give or take 1.5 dB
turn PRESET 1
wait 100
expect kit 1
tap PLAY
wait 4500
expect lufs >-210
expect lufs <-180
tap PLAY
wait 1000
# its accented hits don't clip the USB drum stem
expect stemovers 0
