# The sections' budgets (0.5): the drums 20 %, the synth 30 %, the effects 25 % of a block's time (1161, 1741 and 1451
# of its 5805 us), and the guard: a block that took over 90 % (5224 us) on the first core cuts every drum sound not
# just hit and sheds two voices at once (govguard: its blocks so far). Over its share a section gives up its least important work: the synth's voices are capped at what
# its share buys (a voice over the cap fades out in about 10 ms, whatever its release), the drums' oldest ringing sound
# is cut, a Color strip and then an insert leave the chain and come back when there is room. The simulator's clock is
# the computer's, so the budgets see the times a test gives them (cpufakeN_US: stage N takes US microseconds a block;
# 0 DRUMS 1 SYNTH 2 INSERT+COLOR 3 REVERB+DELAY). govcap: the synth's voices allowed; govshed: voices shed so far;
# govcuts: drum sounds cut so far; govfx: the effects out now (bit t: track t's Color, bit 4 + t: its insert).
#   tools/build.py ../../app --host, then  app/build/host/ghettomachine_host app/tests/budgets.txt OUTDIR
