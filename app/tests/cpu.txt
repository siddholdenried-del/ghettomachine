# CPU (0.5): a track's insert and Color sleep once silence has gone through them a while (fxsleepN: track N's chain
# asleep), and wake with its next sound; SETUP turned with SELECT shows the render's time by stage (cpuN: stage N's
# running mean, us x 10 a block: 0 DRUMS 1 SYNTH 2 INSERT+COLOR 3 REVERB+DELAY 4 MASTER 5 OTHER 6 TOTAL; cpupkN: its
# worst block lately, the tick on its bar; the band's PEAK, the total's). Since the second CPU pass the reverb and delay
# and the master bus run on the second core, a block behind the first (3 and 4 are its time).
#   tools/build.py ../../app --host, then  app/build/host/ghettomachine_host app/tests/cpu.txt OUTDIR (OUTDIR must exist)
