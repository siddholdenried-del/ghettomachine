# The drums' edits are saved (0.5, project format 10): the DrumEngine's patch goes with SAVE and comes back at the
# start, on the kit it was made on. A format-9 project (proj_v9.img, fixtures/make_v9.txt) loads as it was, its drums
# on their kit's presets, and saves as 10. dparamPAD_I: the DrumEngine's parameter I of pad PAD (1..11), its tick
flash_load app/tests/fixtures/proj_v9.img
reboot
wait 600
expect dirty 0
expect projfmt 9
expect hit0_3 1
expect prob0_1 75
expect nsteps3 1
expect demode 1
# the snare's DECAY (its parameter 7) at 0, as its preset has it; turned up: EDIT, page 2, K3
expect dparam2_7 0
turn ALGO -3
wait 50
key 3 down
wait 20
key 3 up
tap EDIT
wait 100
turn SELECT 1
wait 100
turn K3 5
wait 100
expect dparam2_7 >0
expect dirty 1
tap SAVE
wait 400
expect dirty 0
expect projfmt 13
reboot
wait 600
expect projfmt 13
expect dparam2_7 >0
expect hit0_3 1
expect prob0_1 75
expect nsteps3 1
# another kit: the edit isn't that kit's; saved there and back, the kit's own presets
turn PRESET 1
wait 100
tap SEQ
wait 50
turn PRESET 1
wait 100
expect dparam2_7 0
tap SAVE
wait 400
reboot
wait 600
expect dparam2_7 0
