# The FX returns (0.5, for dub): the mixer's RETURNS page (GLO, then SELECT past DELAY): K1 BongVerb's return, K2 its
# decay, K3 BongDelay's return, K4 its feedback: the FX pages' own knobs, one value in two places. The
# returns go on past their old top (127) in quarter-dB steps to +12 dB more (175: +15.5 dB over the preset's 85), shown
# in dB; a saved 0..127 sounds as it did. (fx0 decay, fx3 verb return, fx5 feedback, fx7 delay return)
listen
wait 300
master 4096
turn ALGO 2
wait 60
expect trk 3
tap GLO
wait 60
expect page 9
turn SELECT 4
wait 60
expect mixpage 4
expect fx3 85
expect fx7 85
where
wait 100
said MIXER
# T3 (its reverb send 45): a note, its verb's tail at the preset's return
midi 92 3C 64
wait 300
midi 82 3C 00
wait 400
peakreset
wait 500
expect peak_db_max <-70
# K1 all the way: +15.5 dB, said in dB; the tail as much louder
turn K1 90
wait 400
expect fx3 175
said LEVEL +15.5dB
midi 92 3C 64
wait 300
midi 82 3C 00
wait 400
peakreset
wait 500
expect peak_db_max >-58
# back to the old top (+3.5 dB) and to the preset's (0 dB)
turn K1 -48
wait 60
expect fx3 127
said LEVEL +3.5dB
turn K1 -42
wait 60
expect fx3 85
said LEVEL +0.0dB
