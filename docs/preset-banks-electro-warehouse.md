# Machine Funk and Warehouse Pressure

Two original preset banks for GhettoMachine’s stock synth. Machine Funk is Detroit electro: offbeat bass, short machine chatter and pitched percussion, without shiny lead stacks. Warehouse Pressure is darker and more physical: restrained subs, narrow worn tones, pressure stabs and tuned industrial hits. The seven polys in each bank play the notes you enter; the pitches are not baked-in chords. Each synth patch works dry. Where noted, the sound recalls a light **track** Color preset, not a master effect.
