# Deep House and Dub / Broken Beat

These patches are synthesized from scratch. Four-note chords work best when played as real voicings, not as fixed detuned chords. Try the keys and stabs on the offbeat. Most basses and hits leave Color off; where enabled, Color is a light starting texture, not a substitute for the synth patch. No factory patch opens up a master effect or adds a send. M4 is level calibration on these patches, not a timbre macro.
